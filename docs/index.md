# Tools & Trims

Give your tools a new look with Minecraft's armor trims. Apply trim patterns to swords, pickaxes, axes, shovels, and hoes at a smithing table.

Trims are cosmetic: they change how a tool looks, not its stats.

## What can I trim?

The mod supports swords, pickaxes, axes, shovels, and hoes, with the vanilla trim patterns and materials available in your Minecraft version. Copper tools are supported from Minecraft 1.21.9. Resin trim material is available from Minecraft 1.21.4.

| Minecraft version | Supported tools |
| --- | --- |
| 1.21.1–1.21.3 | Swords, pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes |
| 1.21.4–1.21.8 | Swords, pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes |
| 1.21.9–1.21.11 | Swords, pickaxes, axes, shovels, hoes, including copper tools |

Use the mod file made for your exact Minecraft version.
