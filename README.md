# Customize your tools with all 18 vanilla armor trims

Tools & Trims lets you customize your swords, pickaxes, axes, shovels, and hoes with the existing armor trim patterns.

Use the smithing table to create thousands of unique visual combinations without changing your tools' stats.

---

## Download the mod

The mod is available to download on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/tools-trims), [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/project/tools-trims) and [GitHub](https://github.com/nexi449/ToolsAndTrims).

---

## Features

Tools & Trims gives you full visual customization for your survival tools.

- All 18 vanilla armor trim patterns available for tools.
- Customize swords, pickaxes, axes, shovels, and hoes.
- Use the vanilla smithing table and existing progression.
- Thousands of possible visual combinations.
- Tool stats and gameplay balance remain unchanged.

---

## Compatibility

Tools & Trims currently supports Fabric, with planned expansions for NeoForge, Forge, and Quilt across versions 1.21.1 to 26.3.

Future updates are planned to introduce trims for Maces, Tridents, Bows, and Crossbows, alongside glowing trims, unique custom designs, and additional ways to style your survival gear.
